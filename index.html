AutoWittenberg

AutoWittenberg is a personal automation tool built by the owner of the Reformed Arts Etsy shop. It reads the shop's own listing photos and details from the Etsy API, generates Pinterest-ready pin images and copy, and publishes them to the shop owner's own Pinterest business account via the Pinterest API and to the shop owner's own Instagram professional account via the Instagram API.

This is a single-user, first-party tool: it publishes only Reformed Arts' own original artwork to its own accounts. It does not act on behalf of, or provide any service to, any other user.
