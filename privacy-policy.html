Privacy Policy — AutoWittenberg

Last updated: September 25, 2026

AutoWittenberg is a personal automation tool built and operated by the owner of the Reformed Arts Etsy shop. It is used solely by its owner to publish the shop's own original artwork to the shop owner's own Pinterest business account. AutoWittenberg is not a public product, does not have other users, and does not offer any service to third parties.

What data this app accesses

AutoWittenberg reads listing data (photos, titles, descriptions, and tags) from the Reformed Arts Etsy shop via the Etsy API, and creates Pins on the Reformed Arts Pinterest account via the Pinterest API. It does not access any other Etsy or Pinterest account, and does not collect data about site visitors, customers, or any third party.

What this app does not do

  • It does not collect or store personal information about any individual.
  • It does not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not scrape content from other Etsy or Pinterest accounts.
  • It does not serve ads or use tracking cookies.

Data storage

API credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub Actions environment and are never committed to source code. Generated pin images are stored temporarily and deleted on a rolling basis.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ReformedArts@gmail.com
